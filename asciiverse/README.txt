Cataclysm
=========

Whales' post-apocalyptic roguelike (2010 to 2013), set in the countryside
of New England after a plague of monsters and zombies: an unbounded world
of towns, forests, labs and caves, survival needs, crafting, vehicles,
NPCs and their missions. Its community fork became Cataclysm: Dark Days
Ahead.

This build is the Asciiverse packaging of github.com/Whales/Cataclysm,
from github.com/theasciiverse/cataclysm; PATCHES.md there lists every
change. Cataclysm is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License (LICENSE).

Running it
----------

Start ./cataclysm in a terminal of at least 80x24. The game data in data/
beside the program is copied at each start to your own folder, where the
game plays from and saves: $XDG_STATE_HOME/cataclysm (usually
~/.local/state/cataclysm), or ~/Library/Application Support/cataclysm on
macOS. Options (options.txt) and keys (keymap.txt) are kept there too.

? in the game opens the help, with the keys. S saves and quits. The world
persists between characters: a new character starts in the same world as
the last one. README.upstream is the upstream readme.
